## Bug Fixes

- **Real fonts on the CV** - The Google Fonts import was quietly dropped during the build, so the site and the CV PDF were both falling back to system fonts (the PDF service even further, to Liberation Mono). Inter and Caveat are now bundled with the site itself, so they show up everywhere they should.
- **PDF export uses the right theme** - The CV download was requesting the wrong theme from the PDF service; it now asks for the normal theme explicitly, and the service waits for the fonts to finish loading before printing.

## Behind the scenes

- **`make deploy-pdf`** - A new target rebuilds and redeploys just the PDF service, without touching the rest of the stack.
