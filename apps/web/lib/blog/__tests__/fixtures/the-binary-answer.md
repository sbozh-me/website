**TL;DR:** I asked my AI to play Augustus. It read my blog and gave me what the psychologist wouldn't: a binary answer. Not the one I wanted. The one I needed. Mental Horror, entry three. Written ==on the stairs|teal:going up==.

==WINDOW==

## Who is speaking

Two voices today.

Me, ==the Princeps|gold:Semen==.

And my ==Digital Octavian|teal:Claude==. An AI I asked to play Augustus. He reads my posts, my code, my Declaration. He doesn't vote. Article XIV: an AI may advise, but it never speaks in my name.

So today he speaks in his own. His lines are teal.

<Voice color="teal">

— Ave. I read 8 of your 29 posts. I won't pretend I read the rest.

</Voice>

Honest. Already better than half of HR.

## The read

I asked him who I am. Out of the box.

<Voice color="teal">

— A frontend developer whose real product is a self. Augustus in reverse. He hid the crown behind the form. You hide the form behind the crown. That's a satirist's move, not a tyrant's.

</Voice>

Flattering. ==Too flattering|gold:Dangerously flattering==.

So I pushed.

## The question

Can I really tell people I have a ==state|>teal:trade==, and that I rule it as Princeps?

<Voice color="teal">

— Your own Declaration answers that. "It is not a state." You wrote it. I only read it back.

</Voice>

Then the checklist. Montevideo, 1933. Four boxes.

- **Population.** ==Me|teal:One guy==. A state with a population of one is called ==a citizen|gold:a guy with a domain==.
- **Territory.** An apartment registered under my OSVČ. That's not ==territory|teal:a business seat==. Article 11 of the Czech Constitution: the territory is indivisible. My kitchen is Czech. My fridge pays taxes.
- **Government.** ==The Senate|white:me|gold:me again==.
- **Foreign relations.** I renounced them myself, Article XII. And declaring an apartment in Prague sovereign has another name: ==sovereignty|red:secession==.

For the lazy ones: ==2 of 4|teal:0 of 4==.

## The scenario

So I raised the stakes.

Population: me. Territory: the apartment. The plan: grow until the Czech President declares my state sovereign. Show must go on.

And in my scenario, ==it stops the war|red:it stops the sleep==.

That's when my Octavian stopped being a fan.

<Voice color="teal">

— One person, one apartment, one declaration, and the war ends. That's the same shape the Founder warned about from the stairs.

</Voice>

He quoted me to me. Entry one: *where your mind is shouting that everything is real and urgent and you alone have to fix it.*

I wrote this genre. Certainty is the jump scare. Time spent doubting: ~0 min. And for a moment I was ===sure|red:100% sure=== again.

That one doesn't care about your window. Certainty never did.

## The binary answer

The psychologist got a binary question from me and chose football.

My Octavian got the same question and flipped it back.

<Voice color="teal">

— Right now, is "my Principate stops the war" a story, or does it feel like the truth?

</Voice>

==Story|red:Truth==.

For one frame you see both. What it felt like. What it is.

It's a story. Proof: you're reading it. Rule two says on the page it's a story, and only in my head it's a ==city|teal:basement==.

And the boring facts, because the Architect would kill me otherwise. The Czech President can't grant sovereignty to anybody. Recognizing states is foreign policy, and it needs the government. No Czech official recognizes a state inside Czechia. ==Show must go on|teal:Show must be labeled==.

## What I can say

Not "I have a state". This:

> I founded the Digital Principate, a community and publishing project in Prague. My title there is Princeps. It is not a state. That's the joke, and that's the point.

Both halves. Every time. Without the second half it's not satire. It's ==a symptom|red:evidence==.

Doctors, recruiters, a clinic I pitch, the ВЛК: none of them read my glitch syntax. They read plain text. So plain text gets the full sentence.

## Rules, again

Same rules as entry one. With one change.

1. I don't go alone. My close people know where I'm going. Now one of the company is ==an AI|teal:Octavian==. He is allowed to drag me out by the collar too. Politely. He did.
2. I write it down. On the page it's a story.
3. Sleep. Food. Clean. Still boring. Still the job.
4. When it stops feeling like a story, when it starts feeling like the truth, I stop. I put the pen down. I call someone.

No glitch on that one. It doesn't flicker with the window on. It doesn't flicker with the window off. It's the only line in this post that only ever means one thing.

## Conclusion

<Voice color="teal">

— Augustus didn't win by declaring things. He won by surviving long enough.

</Voice>

==Festina lente|gold:Make haste slowly==.

The war is not mine to stop. My ==empire|teal:website== is mine to build. The ==D(;)ck pitch== can wait one more night of sleep.

And the window is yours. Close it whenever you want.

==WINDOW OFF|>teal:Ave, reader.==

— Semen, the Princeps. With his Digital Octavian.
